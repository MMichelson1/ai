Why we are doing this

The AI Collective is a grassroots community. Every month our Chapters run free meetups and education events where people talk about how AI is changing their lives, and learn to use it safely, properly, and responsibly.

Keep your judgment. Keep your critical thinking and your creativity. Stay in control of AI, and never let AI control you.

That is the human side of AI, and it is what we teach. This campaign tells your city that we are here doing this work for free, and it opens the door for local organizations that want to support it.

Who we are looking for

We are looking for annual sponsors in your market. These are organizations that already support community education and want to be seen doing it. If you know someone at one of these, you are already halfway there.

BanksCredit unionsColleges and universities Law firmsAccounting firmsData centers Power and utility companiesTowns and city halls City clubs and business clubs

Collaborate with other groups

We do media trades with other groups, like TAG, AI Tinkerers, and AI Camp. A media trade is cross promotion with no money involved. We're also open to doing events together.

Reach out to other groups in your town and connect with them. Their events can go on our regional calendar, and they get picked up in the weekly newsletter.

How to post

Plan on about 10 minutes the first time. After that, a new card takes a couple of minutes.

  1. Get your Chapter logos

    The generator loads your logos for you when you pick your Chapter. Download them here for everything else, like slides, Luma pages, and flyers.

    This Chapter is coming soon. Contact the regional office at mark@aicollective.com if you can help get it started.

    Downloads are not available in this view. Press and hold an image (or right-click it) and choose Save Image.

    Color goes on white or light backgrounds. White goes on orange, slate, blue, or a photo. Black is for one-color printing and paperwork.

  2. Open the Campaign Generator

    Open it in your phone or laptop browser and bookmark it. If it opens inside another app and says uploads are blocked, open the link in Safari or Chrome instead.

    Open the Campaign Generator

  3. Make it yours

    Type your Chapter name in step 1 of the generator, spelled the way it appears in the list. Your Chapter logo, your Chapter name, and your member numbers fill in on their own. Every card then names your city, like "Free AI classes in Augusta." Posting for the whole region? Pick Southeast. Posting for the global page? Pick The AI Collective, and the cards name no city at all.

    Add your name and how people reach you, so leads come straight to you. Leave them blank and leads go to the regional office: Mark Michelson, mark@aicollective.com or Calendly.com/michelson. Then add your own event photo, or pick the classroom photo or the social night photo.

  4. Pick a card and a message

    Choose the kind of card along the top of the generator:

    Headshot card. Your photo, your name, your title, and your organization, with a short two-line message. There are more than 30 messages to pick from, or write your own.

    Holiday card. New for the season: Thanksgiving, Happy Holidays, and Happy New Year. Each one comes with its own picture and message, and shows up only in its season. Print the Thanksgiving card for the check-in table at your events this week.

    Luma card. A simple sponsor ask to drop into any Luma event. Whoever runs the event gets the lead.

    Every card comes with post text to copy into the body of your post.

  5. Make a batch of 10 to 15

    Mix it up. Swap the photo, the background, and the message so your feed never repeats. Download each card, and pick the size for where you post: square for LinkedIn, portrait for the Instagram feed, story for Stories.

  6. Post two or three times a week through January 1

    Attach a card, paste the post text, and share it on LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, or wherever your people are. Never post the card alone. Have fun with it.
