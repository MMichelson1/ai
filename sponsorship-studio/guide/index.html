Sponsor Studio Playbook

Set up your chapter's sponsorship toolkit and start landing sponsors — in about 15 minutes. No installs, no logins, works in any browser.

Open the Sponsor Studio → Try the interactive demo

What it is. The AIC Sponsorship Studio turns your chapter's details into everything you need to win sponsors — a branded brochure, a price calculator, and ready-to-send proposals, agreements, and invoices. Enter your information once; it flows into every document, in nine languages.

1

Open the Studio

Where to start

Open the Sponsor Studio in any browser. Everything runs across the tabs along the top — you'll spend most of your time in Settings, Calculator, and Brochure. Switch languages any time from the menu on the right.

2

Set up your chapter

Settings — about 5 minutes

In Settings → Chapter profile, add your details. Everything you type updates the brochure, proposals, and calculator live.

  • Chapter name, members, and event stats — these headline your brochure.
  • Logo & up to 3 event photos — optional, but they make the brochure yours.
  • Referred by — your name and email. Add these so the sponsors you bring are credited to you under the referral program.
  • Sponsor contact defaults to Erich Starrett, who runs the sponsor call and closes — leave it unless your chapter has its own closer.

Back up your setupYour settings live in your own browser. Use Settings → Export to save a small file, and Import to restore it on another computer.

3

Check your rates

Calculator

The Calculator shows your annual partner tiers and event pricing in your currency — a full year of annual tiers is worth $54,000 per chapter at capacity. Tap Add to proposal on any tier to drop it straight into a sponsor proposal.

4

Send your brochure to prospects

Brochure — this is how you sell

Your branded brochure is already built from your Settings. Open the Brochure tab, click Download PDF (8.5×11), and email it to a prospect — or hand it over in person.

Every brochure ends with the call to action below. When a prospect is ready, Book a sponsorship call takes them straight to Erich's calendar, and Referred by keeps the lead credited to you.

5

Turn interest into a proposal

Proposal

When a sponsor is interested, open the Proposal tab, add the tiers and event packages they want, and preview a clean, numbered proposal you can download and send. Agreements and invoices work the same way.

Backed by a campaign

Amplify with social media

The Sponsor Studio is supported by a social-media campaign you run with the AIC Annual Chapter Sponsor Campaign Generator. Use it to spin up ready-to-post announcements and outreach that point prospects to your brochure and booking link — so your outreach and your posts tell the same story.

Suggested rhythm: post to announce you're seeking annual partners, send the brochure to warm prospects, and let the "Book a sponsorship call" button do the scheduling.
