**Subject:** Your new toolkit — the AIC Sponsorship Studio (live now)

Hi everyone,

We've built a tool to make winning and managing sponsorships dramatically easier for your chapter: the **AIC Sponsorship Studio**. It runs entirely in your web browser — nothing to install, no login.

**What it is:** a single, self-contained sponsorship toolkit tailored to The AI Collective. You enter your chapter's details once, and it powers everything below from that — in **English, Español, Français, Deutsch, Português, 日本語, Italiano, العربية, and Svenska**.

**What it does:**
- **Sponsorship Calculator** — instant, consistent pricing for annual tiers, event sponsorships, and newsletter placements, in your local currency.
- **Brochure** — a polished, chapter-branded sponsor brochure you can **download as an 8.5×11 PDF** and send to prospects.
- **Proposals & Agreements** — generate sponsor proposals, agreements, and invoices in a few clicks.
- **Everything stays in sync** — change a number in Settings and it updates across the brochure, proposals, and calculator automatically.

**Getting started (about 5 minutes):**
1. **Open the Studio** → https://mmichelson1.github.io/ai/sponsorship-studio/
2. **Settings** — add your chapter info: name, member count, logo, and event stats.
3. **Calculator** — review your sponsorship rates and tiers.
4. **Brochure** — your branded brochure is ready; click **Download PDF** to share it with prospects. You can also add up to **3 of your own event photos** in Settings.

**Who handles the sponsor call — and getting credit.** By default, the brochure's "Book a sponsorship call" button routes prospects to **Erich Starrett, Director of Sponsor Partnerships**, who runs the conversation and closes the deal — so you don't have to. Leave that default in place unless your chapter has its own trained closer (then set your own Sponsor contact in Settings). Whenever you send a brochure, add **your name and email in the "Referred by" field** so the leads you bring are credited to you under the referral program.
