AIC SPONSORSHIP STUDIO — SETUP KIT
For regional managers and chapter leads
===========================================================

WHAT'S IN THIS KIT
-------------------
1. AIC-Sponsorship-Studio.html  — The tool. A sponsorship brochure,
   proposal builder, agreement/invoice generator, and price calculator,
   all in one file. Runs offline in any web browser.

2. AIC-Studio-Setup-Guide.html  — A one-page visual setup guide.
   Switch languages in the top-right (English, Español, Français,
   Deutsch, Português, 日本語).

3. START HERE - Instructions.txt — This file.


SETUP IN 4 STEPS (about 5 minutes)
-----------------------------------
1. OPEN IT
   Save "AIC-Sponsorship-Studio.html" to your computer and double-click
   it. It opens in any browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox) and works
   fully offline. Nothing to install, no login.

2. SET YOUR CHAPTER CTA LINK  ***REQUIRED***
   Go to  Settings -> Chapter profile  and fill in "Brochure CTA link"
   with your chapter's Luma / signup URL. This is where the brochure's
   "Sponsor" button sends prospects. Until it is set, that button has
   nowhere to go and a warning stays on screen.

   >> Every chapter and region must set its own CTA link. The kit ships
      blank on purpose — the brochure is not ready to share until yours
      is filled in.

3. FILL IN YOUR CHAPTER DETAILS
   Still in Settings, add your chapter name, member count, contact email,
   logo, and event stats. Everything you enter updates the brochure,
   proposals, and calculator live.

4. BACK UP YOUR SETUP
   Use  Settings -> Export  to download a small config file. Keep it
   safe — Settings -> Import restores everything instantly on another
   computer or after clearing your browser.


HOW YOUR DATA IS STORED
------------------------
Everything you type is saved only in your own browser, on your own
device. Nothing is uploaded or shared automatically, and no other lead
sees your settings. That is why backing up (Step 4) matters: if you
switch devices or clear your browser, re-import your config file to pick
up where you left off.


FOR REGIONAL MANAGERS
----------------------
- Forward this whole kit (all files) to each chapter lead in your region.
- Remind every lead to complete Step 2 (the CTA link) before they share
  their brochure — it is the one step that cannot be skipped.
- Each lead's setup is independent. If you want a consistent regional
  baseline, configure one copy, use Settings -> Export, and share that
  config file for leads to Import and then adjust.
