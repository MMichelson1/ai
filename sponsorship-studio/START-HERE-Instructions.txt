AIC SPONSORSHIP STUDIO — SETUP KIT
For regional managers and chapter leads
===========================================================

WHAT THIS IS
------------
The AIC Sponsorship Studio is a browser tool for building sponsorship
brochures, proposals, agreements/invoices, and price quotes — all from
your chapter's own settings, in nine languages. Nothing to install.


HOW TO OPEN IT
--------------
1. LIVE LINK (use this):
   https://mmichelson1.github.io/ai/sponsorship-studio/
   Open it in any browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox). You always get
   the latest version, and your settings are saved in your own browser.

2. OFFLINE BACKUP:
   A copy of "AIC-Sponsorship-Studio.html" is kept in the shared Drive
   folder. Download it and double-click to run fully offline if needed.


SETUP IN 3 STEPS (about 5 minutes)
-----------------------------------
1. SETTINGS — set up your chapter
   Open Settings and add your chapter name, member count, logo, and event
   stats. Everything you enter updates the brochure, proposals, and
   calculator live.

2. SPONSOR CONTACT (optional)
   The brochure's "Book a sponsorship call" button defaults to the AIC
   sponsor-partnerships contact. To route prospects to you instead, add
   your own Sponsor contact in Settings — an email, a calendar link
   (e.g. Calendly), or both. You can also add an optional "Referred by"
   name/email so qualified leads are credited to you.

3. BACK UP YOUR SETUP
   Use  Settings -> Export  to download a small config file. Keep it safe —
   Settings -> Import restores everything instantly on another computer or
   after clearing your browser.


HOW YOUR DATA IS STORED
------------------------
Everything you type is saved only in your own browser, on your own device.
Nothing is uploaded or shared automatically, and no other lead sees your
settings. That is why backing up (Step 3) matters: if you switch devices or
clear your browser, re-import your config file to pick up where you left off.


FOR REGIONAL MANAGERS
----------------------
- Send each chapter lead the live link above.
- Each lead's setup is independent. If you want a consistent regional
  baseline, configure one copy, use Settings -> Export, and share that
  config file for leads to Import and then adjust.
